The exercises across the provided modules utilize a wide range of Arabic words to practice grammar, sentence construction, reading, and translation. I have extracted the active vocabulary used specifically within the exercise sentences, fill-in-the-blanks, translation practices, and sentence trees, deliberately excluding the dedicated vocabulary memorization tables. 

Here is the comprehensive categorized list of words applied in the exercises:

### **1. Demonstratives (إسْمُ الإِشَارَةِ)**
These words are frequently used in the exercises to point out objects and people:
*   **هذا** (This - masculine)
*   **هذه** (This - feminine)
*   **ذلك** (That - masculine)
*   **تلك** (That - feminine)

### **2. Pronouns (الضَّمَائِر)**
Personal pronouns are heavily practiced in the dialogue and sentence-building exercises:
*   **أنا** (I)
*   **نحن** (We)
*   **أنتَ** (You - masculine singular)
*   **أنتِ** (You - feminine singular)
*   **أنتما** (You two - dual)
*   **أنتم** (You all - masculine plural)
*   **أنتن** (You all - feminine plural)
*   **هو** (He / It - masculine)
*   **هي** (She / It - feminine)
*   **هم** (They - masculine plural)
*   **هن** (They - feminine plural)

### **3. Question Words (أَسْمَاءُ الاِسْتِفْهَام)**
The exercises use these particles to form interrogative sentences:
*   **ما / ماذا** (What)
*   **مَن** (Who)
*   **هل / أ** (Is / Are / Do / Does - used for yes/no questions)
*   **أين** (Where)
*   **كيف** (How)
*   **أيّ / أيّة** (Which)
*   **متى** (When)
*   **لِماذا** (Why)

### **4. Prepositions & Words of Place/Time (حُرُوفُ الجَرّ وَ الظُّرُوف)**
